# 🌿 Arovia Health App - Complete User Guide

## 📱 How to Use Each Feature

---

## 🍦 **Food Tracking with Image Recognition**

### How It Works:
1. **Tap "📸 Take Photo" or "🖼️ Select Photo"**
   - Camera opens to take a photo of your food
   - Or pick an existing photo from gallery

2. **Type the Food Name**
   - Start typing: "arun", "baskin", "vadilal", "amul", etc.
   - For ice creams, type the brand name for suggestions

3. **Ice Cream Detection** 
   - When you type ice cream brands, you'll see:
     - All brands (Arun, Baskin Robbins, Vadilal, Amul, Mother Dairy, Haagen Dazs)
     - Different flavors per brand
     - **Exact calories for each flavor and scoop count**

### Example: Arun Ice Cream
- **Arun Butterscotch**: 120 cal per scoop
- **Arun Vanilla**: 110 cal per scoop
- **Arun Chocolate**: 130 cal per scoop
- Different from **Baskin Robbins Butterscotch** (150 cal)

### Example: Vadilal Ice Cream
- **Vadilal Butterscotch**: 115 cal
- **Vadilal Fruit & Nut**: 140 cal (more fiber!)
- **Vadilal Mango**: 120 cal

4. **Select Quantity**
   - Enter number of scoops: "1", "2", "3"
   - Total calories auto-calculate

5. **Get Notification**
   - 🔔 Instant notification if food is junk
   - 💪 Suggestion to exercise to burn calories

## 👟 **Step Tracker - How It Works**

### What It Does:
- **Automatically counts** your steps throughout the day (using phone sensor)
- **Resets daily** at midnight
- **Calculates calories burned** (approximately 0.05 cal per step)

### How to Use:
1. **Enable Location & Health Permissions**
   - Go to Settings → App Permissions → Arovia
   - Enable "Body Sensors" or "Activity"

2. **Open Analytics Tab**
   - See **Today's Steps** in real-time
   - See **Average Steps** (last 7 days)
   - See **Calories Burnt from Steps**

3. **Daily Goal: 10,000 steps**
   - Aim for 10,000 steps = ~500 calories burned
   - 5,000 steps = ~250 calories burned
   - 20,000 steps = ~1000 calories burned

### Example:
- You eat **500 cal ice cream**
- You walk **10,000 steps** = **500 cal burned**
- Net impact = **Balanced!** ✅

## 🔔 **Notifications - How to Receive**

### Types of Notifications:

#### 1. **Automatic Smart Notifications** (at scheduled times)
- **🌅 7:00 AM** - Morning Goal Reminder
  - "Set today's fitness goals"
  
- **💪 5:00 PM** - Exercise Time
  - "Have you worked out today?"
  
- **📊 8:00 PM** - Evening Summary
  - "Check your daily progress"

#### 2. **Instant Notifications** (when you log junk food)
- When you add **ice cream or junk food**
- Shows calorie count
- Suggests exercise to balance

### Why You Might NOT Be Receiving:
1. **Notifications disabled in app settings** → Enable them
2. **Notifications disabled for Arovia app** → Enable in phone settings:
   - Settings → Apps → Arovia → Notifications → Toggle ON
3. **DND (Do Not Disturb) enabled** → Disable temporarily
4. **Battery Saver on** → May delay notifications

### How to Enable Notifications:

**Android:**
- Settings → Apps → Arovia → Permissions → Allow notifications
- Or: Settings → Notifications → Arovia → Allow

**iOS:**
- Settings → Notifications → Arovia
- Toggle "Allow Notifications"
- Allow "Sound", "Badge", "Alert"

## 📊 **Analytics Tab - What You See**

### 👟 Step Tracker Section
- **Today's Steps**: Real-time count
- **Average Steps**: Last 7 days average
- **Calories Burnt**: From step activity

### 🍽️ Food Tracker Section
- **Today's Calories**: Current vs 2000 cal goal
- **Fiber**: Current vs 25g goal
- **Healthy Items**: Count of healthy foods
- **Junk Items**: Count of junk foods

### 📊 Weekly Summary
- **Weekly Steps**: Total steps last 7 days
- **Weekly Calories**: Total calories last 7 days
- **Weekly Fiber**: Total fiber last 7 days

## 👥 **Multi-Profile Feature**

### Benefits:
- Each family member has **separate data**
- Separate food logs
- Separate step counts
- Separate analytics

### How It Works:
1. **Create Profiles**
   - Mom, Dad, Child1, Child2, etc.

2. **Switch Profiles**
   - Home screen → Select different profile
   - Each profile tracks independently

3. **No Data Mixing**
   - Mom's ice cream log ≠ Dad's ice cream log
   - Child's steps ≠ Parent's steps

## 🎯 **Quick Tips**

### For Weight Loss:
- Track all food with exact calories
- Aim for > 10,000 steps daily
- Keep junk food to minimum (max 1-2 items/week)

### For Fitness:
- Use step tracker to monitor daily movement
- Combine steps with exercise
- Balance calories eaten vs calories burned

### For Health:
- Reach 25g fiber daily (green veggies, fruits)
- Eat > 5 healthy foods daily
- Keep junk intake low

## ❓ **FAQs**

**Q: Why is step count 0?**
A: 
- App not initialized (restart phone)
- Health permission not granted
- Try walking a few steps to activate sensor

**Q: Can I edit a food log entry?**
A: Currently, delete and re-add with correct info

**Q: Do notifications work offline?**
A: Scheduled notifications work offline. Instant ones need internet.

**Q: Why are calories not exact?**
A: Different serving sizes vary. Update quantity field.

**Q: Can I set custom daily goals?**
A: Calorie goal: 2000 (default)
   Fiber goal: 25g (default)

## 📲 **Need Help?**

- **App crashes**: Close and restart
- **Blank screen**: Force stop app and restart
- **Notifications not working**: Check phone settings
- **Step count wrong**: Calibrate by walking
